# BCNW Question 5.7 — statement audit

Source: [arXiv:2004.10227v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2004.10227v3), §§2.1, 2.2, 4.1, Question 5.7 (§5.1).

- Native Mathlib left `Quandle` and `ShelfHom`; left translations are permutations.
- The common BCNW core uses the subgroup generated by left translations in a subquandle, acting in the ambient quandle. Its restriction to the subquandle is the intrinsic inner group action. The intersection in `orbitIn` is therefore harmless for the subquandles and anchors in the series.
- The article defines extension closure using congruences. By the correspondence in §2.2 this is equivalent to testing surjective homomorphisms and all full fibres. A shelf homomorphism between quandles also preserves inverse translations by bijectivity. Each fibre is a subquandle by idempotency.
- The source uses all orbit series and equality of successive terms, not singleton stabilization or only principal series.
- `OSomega` uses a positive finite bound, as in the source. The fibre bound may depend on the fibre. No finiteness, connectedness, or common fibre bound is required.
- Nonempty source and base are explicit, consistent with the usual quandle convention. Empty quandles (if admitted) give vacuous orbit-series conditions and no additional obstruction to extension closure.
- The two types have independent universe parameters. The original question asks two independent yes/no questions; `answer` specifies the proposed yes/no pair without proving it.
